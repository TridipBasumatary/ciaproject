Category 02

Hero changes — decode

For every hero entry, record: hero name, skill/stat changed, old value, new value, and official patch source. Then explain the practical effect in your own words.

What the patch says

Paste the verified official adjustment here. Example format: “Skill 1 damage: old value → new value.” Do not publish example values as real patch data.

What it could mean

Explain whether it might affect damage, cooldown, survivability, combos, lane matchups, or team fights. Mark this as analysis until verified with gameplay or data.

Category 05

Map & battlefield changes

Cover Turtle and Lord mechanics, jungle camps, minions, turrets, terrain, and other battlefield rules when they appear in official notes.

Decode checklist

  • What mechanic changed?
  • What was the old rule and what is the new rule?
  • Could timing, rotations, positioning, or objective control be affected?
  • What evidence would confirm that interpretation?

How this site can be evaluated

Usability KPIs

  • Task completion rate: users who find the requested change.
  • Time to find: seconds needed to locate a patch topic.
  • Source click-through: users who open a reference link.
  • Comprehension: users who correctly explain a change.

Research integrity

Distinguish official patch facts from your interpretation. Do not claim the website tracks real visitor behaviour unless you actually add and configure analytics.
