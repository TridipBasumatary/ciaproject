Tank Junglers became too efficient after the previous jungle changes, so their clear speed and early durability were pulled back.

Stronger early-to-mid game combat and laning.

M
MLBB BUFF

2.1.90

Skill 2 mana cost: 90 → 70.

Battlefield & systems

  • Thunder Fenrir and Molten Fiend base Gold reward: +20%.
  • Horned Lizard, Fire Beetle and Lava Golem base Gold reward: +15%.
  • Mythical Honor Melissa Skill 1 cooldown: 4.5–3s → 4–2.5s.
  • Atlas Skill 1 cooldown behaviour was fixed to the intended previous-patch value.
PATCH 2.1.95 · S41

Kaja Revamp + Chaos Clash

4 Aug 2026 · Original Server

Kaja received a major kit redesign and Chaos Clash returned with a rebuilt map and new rules.
G
MLBB NERF

Gord

2.1.95

Early-to-mid game damage was reduced.

N
MLBB BUFF

Novaria

2.1.95

Skill 2 was made easier to use effectively without removing its high-skill ceiling.

Battlefield & systems

  • Chaos Clash's map was rebuilt with new visuals, spawn points, bushes and central obstacles.
  • The final safe zone is randomly generated.
  • Guest Heroes and hero-specific buffs can appear.
  • Component items were removed from the mode.
  • Final equipment was standardised around 2,000 Gold.
  • Power Potion, Magic Potion and Rock Potion were replaced by stackable Chaos Potion choices.

PATCH 2.1.95b · S41

Map Cleanup + Shop Reorganisation

9 Sep 2026 · Original Server

Annual map changes were removed before Season 42, shop tabs were simplified, and Chaos Clash received a fix.
No hero-specific change was recorded in this patch.

Battlefield & systems

  • Broken Walls, Dangerous Grass, Flying Cloud and Expanding Rivers were scheduled for removal on 15 September server time.
  • Special, Preparation and Fragments shop tabs were merged into Other; item availability and purchase methods were not changed.
  • Khufra was temporarily disabled in Chaos Clash because of a mode-specific bug.

04 · Battlefield

Map & system changes

Only changes recorded in the supplied six-month patch reference are shown here.

🗺️ Major map moments

  • 2.1.95: Chaos Clash returned with a redesigned map, random final safe zone, Guest Heroes and hero-specific buffs.
  • 2.1.95b: Broken Walls, Dangerous Grass, Flying Cloud and Expanding Rivers were scheduled for removal before S42.
  • 2.2.16: Revealing Wisps, Healing Turtle and Golden Turret arrived; Flying Cloud returned with upgraded visuals.

💰 Economy moments

  • 2.1.90: selected jungle resource Gold rewards increased.
  • 2.2.16: selected jungle Gold rewards fell 13%; comeback-kill Gold threshold changed 2,000 → 1,500.
  • 2.2.16: minion Gold growth was slightly reduced.
