PATCH 2.1.67 · S40

Aulus Revamp + Gold Lane Rebalance

22 April 2026 · Original Server

Aulus was rebuilt for sustained fighting, while Gold Lane Marksmen were rebalanced to reduce extreme early pressure and smooth out scaling, durability, movement speed and mana costs.

Player takeaway: If you play Gold Lane, expect less free early survivability on some picks and pay closer attention to mana use and scaling. Aulus players should practise chaining skills into Basic Attacks to build sustained pressure.

A
PATCH 2.1.67REVAMP

Aulus

Fighting Spirit now fuels enhanced attacks. His skills were redesigned to link more naturally with Basic Attacks, shifting him toward repeated combat instead of a simple burst pattern.

B
PATCH 2.1.67BUFF

Bruno, Dyrroth, Harith, Melissa, Terizla & Wanwan

These heroes were listed for buffs. Treat this as a direction-of-change summary; the supplied reference does not provide individual numeric values for each one.

C
PATCH 2.1.67NERF

Claude, Hanabi, Karrie, Natan & Sora

These heroes were listed for nerfs as part of the Gold Lane balance pass.

B
PATCH 2.1.67ADJUST

Bane, Beatrix, Brody, Cici, Clint, Irithel, Ixia, Layla, Lesley, Marcel, Miya, Moskov, Obsidia, Popol and Kupa

A broad adjustment group. The patch aimed to reduce overbearing early-lane strength and tune durability, growth, movement speed or mana costs rather than applying one identical change to everyone.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • Gold Lane Marksmen with strong early pressure were generally given less raw durability.
  • High-range or high-mobility Marksmen were also tuned through growth and movement-speed values.
  • Many Marksmen's mana costs were moved closer to a common baseline.
  • The reference confirms additional battlefield, system and event adjustments, but does not enumerate each one.
No season reward was specifically recorded for this update.
PATCH 2.1.67a · S40

Follow-up Hero Balance

13 May 2026 · Original Server

A smaller follow-up focused on Roaming Tank survivability and quality-of-life improvements for Aulus, Chou and Paquito, while reducing Harley's burst damage.

Player takeaway: Chou and Paquito should feel smoother to combo with. Harley's Skill 1 hurts less, while Aulus players need to manage the lower stack cap and cannot rely on the same Ultimate immunity window.

A
PATCH 2.1.67aBUFF / ADJUST

Aulus

Fighting Spirit maximum stacks changed from 6 to 4. His Ultimate lost Control Immunity during the wider slash, so timing and positioning matter more.

B
PATCH 2.1.67aBUFF

Belerick

Skill 1 taunt delay shortened from 1.0s to 0.8s, helping the crowd-control effect land sooner.

C
PATCH 2.1.67aBUFF

Chou

Skill 1 no longer has its backswing, letting Chou Basic Attack sooner after using it. Ultimate cooldown changed from a flat 34s to 36–30s by level, with higher base and chase damage.

H
PATCH 2.1.67aNERF

Harley

Skill 1 card damage reduced from 130–210 to 120–180, lowering his burst damage.

P
PATCH 2.1.67aBUFF

Paquito

Enhanced skills received better movement, damage reduction and lane-pushing interactions, improving the flow of his combos.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • This was primarily a hero balance and feel/response patch; no separate map or system changes were detailed in the supplied reference.
No season reward was specifically recorded for this update.
PATCH 2.1.88 · S41

Hirara Arrives + Roam/Jungle Rework

17 June 2026 · Original Server

Hirara joined the roster, traditional Roam heroes received help, and jungle pacing was changed to make invasions less punishing for teams that fall behind.

Player takeaway: Hirara rewards movement and attack-pattern decisions. Roam players can reconsider traditional Tanks and backline Supports, while junglers should expect more flexible farming and a better chance to recover after an invasion.

H
PATCH 2.1.88NEW HERO

Hirara

Scarlet Shadow Assassin wielding the Twin Fans: Ukifune. Meisen-e allows up to two joystick dashes; Kaerazu sends a fan-shaped Crimson Maple attack and strikes the area again; Infernal Torrent provides major finishing and area pressure.

H
PATCH 2.1.88ADJUST

Hanzo

His jungle identity was pushed toward objective control. HP and attack-speed growth were adjusted to balance the added objective-focused strength.

Y
PATCH 2.1.88BUFF

Yin

Improvements aimed to make his Ultimate/domain combat feel more like a Fighter, supporting direct combat inside his isolated fight.

T
PATCH 2.1.88BUFF DIRECTION

Traditional Roam Tanks & backline Supports

The patch broadly helped older roaming picks so they could compete better against the current roaming meta. Individual values are not specified in the reference.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • Jungle pacing was changed to make leading teams' farming routes more flexible.
  • Players behind in the jungle received more opportunity to recover after an invasion.
  • These comeback/farming changes were followed by Patch 2.1.90's correction to Tank Junglers that became too efficient.

Season rewards

  • Season 41 skin: Zhask — Icespawn Scourge.
  • Season 41 avatar border: Scarlet Embers.
PATCH 2.1.90 · S41

Tank Jungle Correction

2 July 2026 · Original Server

After the jungle changes, some Tank Junglers cleared too efficiently and stayed too durable. This patch slowed selected clears while improving several Fighters and other heroes.

Player takeaway: Tank junglers should expect slower clears or less early sustain. At the same time, the listed jungle monsters pay more Gold, and Melissa, Nolan and Minsitthar gain specific efficiency improvements.

A
PATCH 2.1.90NERF / ADJUST

Akai

Marked targets now consume the mark when hit by a Basic Attack; single-hit damage was increased as compensation. Track the mark rather than assuming it stays active.

B
PATCH 2.1.90NERF

Baxia

Damage against jungle creeps was reduced, slowing his jungle clear.

F
PATCH 2.1.90NERF

Fredrinn

Early-game durability and sustain were reduced, making early fights and farming less forgiving.

A
PATCH 2.1.90BUFF

Argus, Aulus & Esmeralda

The reference lists stronger early-to-mid combat/laning for Argus, improved sustain/laning for Aulus, and improved combat sustainability for Esmeralda.

N
PATCH 2.1.90BUFF

Nolan

Energy restored by hitting a Rift increased from 20 to 25, giving more energy back from that interaction.

M
PATCH 2.1.90BUFF

Minsitthar

Ultimate cooldown became 45s at every level instead of 55–45s. Its damage ratio changed from a flat 40% to 30–50% by level.

M
PATCH 2.1.90BUFF

Melissa

Skill 2 mana cost fell from 90 to 70, making repeated use less mana-intensive.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • Thunder Fenrir and Molten Fiend base Gold rewards increased by 20%.
  • Horned Lizard, Fire Beetle and Lava Golem base Gold rewards increased by 15%, improving income from those jungle resources.
  • In Mythical Honor, Melissa Skill 1 cooldown changed from 4.5–3s to 4–2.5s.
  • Atlas Skill 1 cooldown behaviour was fixed to match the intended value from the previous patch.
No season reward was specifically recorded for this update.
PATCH 2.1.95 · S41

Kaja Revamp + Chaos Clash

4 August 2026 · Original Server

Kaja received a major kit redesign focused on mobility and area control. Chaos Clash returned with a redesigned arena, changing safe-zone behaviour, guest heroes and a simplified equipment setup.

Player takeaway: Kaja now offers more ways to enter, disrupt and control fights instead of relying only on one pick-off. In Chaos Clash, scout the safe zone and available mode bonuses early; old potion and component-item assumptions no longer apply.

K
PATCH 2.1.95REVAMP

Kaja

Passive — Wings of Lightning: skill damage applies Paralysis, briefly slowing enemies and reducing their damage. Skill 1 — Ring of Order: area Primal Lightning damage plus HP recovery. Skill 2 — Soaring Wings: dash, knockback and a soaring state that changes cooldown behaviour. Ultimate — Divine Binding: can restrain and drag multiple enemies in an area.

G
PATCH 2.1.95NERF

Gord

Early-to-mid-game damage was reduced, weakening his pressure before the late game.

N
PATCH 2.1.95BUFF

Novaria

Skill 2 was made easier to use effectively while keeping its high-skill ceiling.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • Chaos Clash's map was rebuilt with new visuals, redistributed spawn points, bushes and central obstacles.
  • The final safe zone is generated randomly instead of always shrinking toward the centre, so teams must adapt to each match.
  • Guest Heroes and hero-specific buffs can appear, adding match-to-match variation.
  • Component items were removed from the mode; final equipment was standardised around 2,000 Gold and attributes were rebalanced.
  • Power Potion, Magic Potion and Rock Potion were removed. Stackable Chaos Potion choices replaced them, offering random stat options.
No season reward was specifically recorded for this update.
PATCH 2.1.95a · S41

Atlas / Hanzo Follow-up

25/26 August 2026 · Original Server

A small balance pass based on ranked and professional-match data. Atlas mobility was reduced, Hanzo was moved away from tank-style sustain, and Hayabusa and Obsidia received targeted improvements.

Player takeaway: Atlas players need to plan engages with less movement speed and a longer cooldown. Hanzo is less suited to relying on tank-style sustain, while Hayabusa and Obsidia received performance help.

A
PATCH 2.1.95aNERF

Atlas

Skill 2 Movement Speed bonus fell from 40% to 30%, and cooldown increased from 12–10s to 13–11s. He has less speed and slightly less frequent access to the skill.

H
PATCH 2.1.95aADJUST

Hanzo

Tank-oriented sustain was reduced, and HP regeneration shifted toward Physical Attack scaling. Building damage becomes more relevant to his recovery.

H
PATCH 2.1.95aBUFF

Hayabusa

Targeted combat-performance improvements were included; the reference does not specify exact numerical values.

O
PATCH 2.1.95aBUFF

Obsidia

Targeted improvements based on recent performance were included; exact numerical values are not listed in the reference.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • Thunder Belt interaction restrictions were adjusted to prevent unintended or overly efficient tank-style scaling.
No season reward was specifically recorded for this update.
PATCH 2.1.95b · S41

Map Cleanup + Shop Reorganisation

9 September 2026 · Original Server

The annual map modifiers were scheduled to end before Season 42, the shop navigation was simplified, and a Chaos Clash bug led to a temporary hero restriction.

Player takeaway: This patch is mostly about the environment and interface, not hero power. If you could not find a shop category, check Other; the tab moved, but the items and purchase methods stayed the same.

No hero-specific changes were recorded for this update.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • On 15 September server time, the active annual map changes were scheduled for removal: Broken Walls, Dangerous Grass, Flying Cloud and Expanding Rivers.
  • The reference says the battlefield would temporarily have no map changes after removal, pending future announcements.
  • Special, Preparation and Fragments shop tabs were merged into the Other tab. The items and purchase methods themselves did not change.
  • Khufra was temporarily disabled in Chaos Clash because of a mode-specific bug.
No season reward was specifically recorded for this update.
PATCH 2.2.16 · S42

10th Anniversary + Masha/Bruno Revamps

16 September 2026 · Original Server

Season 42 and the Starward Decade anniversary began, Masha and Bruno received major redesigns, and new annual map features and economy adjustments changed battlefield decisions.

Player takeaway: Masha is now oriented toward sustained, high-skill brawling, while Bruno revolves around keeping powerballs in play. Watch the new map vision/healing/turret mechanics and remember that jungle/minion income and comeback thresholds changed.

M
PATCH 2.2.16REVAMP

Masha

Primal Pact keeps her three HP bars central. She builds Feral state through combat and uses enemy Wounds to trigger stronger claw attacks. Skill 1 — Feral Swipe deals forward damage and slows; Skill 2 — Claw Strike is a dash/claw attack with a cooldown reset tied to losing a life; Savage Maul was rebuilt for sustained fighting.

B
PATCH 2.2.16REVAMP

Bruno

The redesign centres on repeated powerball play and stronger interaction between skills and Basic Attacks, letting him maintain pressure through continuous kicks. An anniversary art refresh also arrived.

M
PATCH 2.2.16VISUAL UPDATE

Masha, Bruno, Paquito, Kadita, Clint, Luo Yi, Brody & Badang

The anniversary patch included hero art/visual overhauls for these heroes; these are visual updates, not automatically gameplay buffs.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • From 28 September, annual map changes introduced Revealing Wisps (vision/reveal interaction), Healing Turtle (Turtle-related healing feature) and Golden Turret (new turret-related feature). Flying Cloud returned with upgraded visuals.
  • Base Gold from Horned Lizard, Fire Beetle and Lava Golem was reduced by 13%.
  • The Gold deficit required for bonus Gold from enemy hero kills changed from 2,000 to 1,500, making comeback bonus Gold available at a smaller deficit.
  • Minion Gold growth over time was slightly reduced.
  • Mythical Honor battlefield values were adjusted toward standard battlefield values in relevant areas.
  • Sacred Hammer cooldown changed from 16–13s to 13–10s.

Season rewards

  • Season 42 skin: Popol and Kupa — Flamehide Thane.
  • Season 42 avatar border: Starward Decade.
PATCH 2.2.16a · S42

Aulus / Hirara Follow-up Balance

14 October 2026 · Original Server

A separate mini-patch rebalanced Aulus and Hirara and made follow-up adjustments to Eudora, Rafaela and Kalea. The supplied reference cross-checks this entry against patch archives because it is not separately visible in the migrated official news index.

Player takeaway: Aulus has less sustained basic-attack scaling and regeneration, but a more responsive Ultimate. Hirara has fewer opportunities to become untargetable; Eudora, Rafaela and Kalea were also tuned, though the reference does not provide their exact numbers.

A
PATCH 2.2.16aNERF + ULTIMATE BUFF

Aulus

Enhanced Basic Attack scaling and HP regeneration were reduced; Skill 1 cooldown became 15s at all levels. His Ultimate's low-end base damage was raised through redistributed ranges and the final-hit foreswing was shortened.

H
PATCH 2.2.16aNERF

Hirara

Invincibility/untargetability access was reduced, limiting how frequently she can use that defensive window.

E
PATCH 2.2.16aADJUST

Eudora

Follow-up balance intended to keep burst damage and control in check; the supplied reference does not specify exact values.

R
PATCH 2.2.16aADJUST

Rafaela

Healing and support values were rebalanced; exact numerical values are not specified in the reference.

K
PATCH 2.2.16aBUFF

Kalea

Improvements aimed at making her Support/Fighter kit more reliable; exact numerical values are not specified in the reference.

Battlefield, systems & economy

  • No separate map or economy changes were itemised for this mini-patch in the supplied reference.
No season reward was specifically recorded for this update.
